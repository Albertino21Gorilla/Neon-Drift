Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Neon Drift is an independent browser game maintained by AlbertinoTheCoder. For privacy questions or account-data requests, contact neondriftbusiness@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

When you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase authenticate your account. Basic account information can include your Google identifier, email address, name and profile picture. The game uses your account identifier to associate saved progress with you and may display your name. Neon Drift does not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail messages, contacts or Drive files.

The online game stores account-linked points, energy, lifetime points, best score, owned items, equipped items, multiplier tiers and whether you have completed a run. It also records run identifiers, start times, difficulty, multiplier snapshots and completion status to manage rewards and duplicate submissions. Levels and score achievements are calculated from saved progression.

Why this information is used

Account information is used to sign you in, load your progress, save completed-run rewards, process in-game purchases and check requests. Authentication and technical records also help operate and troubleshoot the service. The game code does not include advertising or analytics trackers and does not implement a sale of Google account data.

Browser storage

The local version stores progress and preferences in your browser. Online sign-in stores session information in browser storage so you can stay signed in. These session records are separate from server-held balances. Practice mode keeps its game progress in memory only. Local saves are not imported into online accounts. Clearing browser storage removes local saves and can sign you out, but does not delete your server account.

Services involved

Google provides sign-in, Supabase provides authentication and the database, and GitHub Pages hosts the website. The online game loads its Supabase JavaScript library through esm.sh. Requests to these providers can expose technical information such as IP address, browser information and request timestamps to them. Provider processing, infrastructure, logs and backups are subject to their respective policies; processing locations may differ from your country.

See Google's privacy policy, Supabase's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention and your choices

Account progress currently has no automatic expiry. Run records older than one day are cleaned up when that account starts another run; an unfinished run is replaced when a new one starts. Provider logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules.

You can play the local version without signing in, sign out of the online version, or remove Neon Drift's access in your Google account settings. Removing Google access does not automatically delete previously saved game data. To request access, correction or deletion of your online account data, use the contact email above. Identity verification may be needed to protect your account. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of your data or complain to your local data-protection authority.
