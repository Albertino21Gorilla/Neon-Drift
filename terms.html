You can play the local version without an account. Online play requires Google sign-in and an eligible Google account. Use only accounts you are authorized to use and protect your login credentials. Follow any age and parental-permission requirements applicable to your account and location.

Do not access other players' accounts, interfere with the service, bypass account access controls or submit fabricated rewards to the online game. Please report bugs through the contact email rather than exploiting them against other players or the service.

Virtual items and progress

Points, energy, abilities, skins and trails are game features, not real money. The current game does not sell these items for real money or offer cash redemption. Local progress belongs to the browser save; online progress is separate and depends on successful server saves.

Online runs save when completed. Wait for the save confirmation before leaving. Unfinished runs, connection failures and clearing browser data can cause loss of progress. Practice runs do not award online currency.
